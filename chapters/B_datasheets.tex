\chapter{Component datasheets}
\label{app:datasheets}

This appendix gives the links to the datasheets of the components of the
power stage. They are the 30 parts of \cref{tab:components}, plus the
mechanical parts of the cooling. Where the manufacturer has no stable link to
the PDF file, the link points to the product page, which holds the datasheet
and the other documents of the part. The links were checked on 3 November
2026.
